# Fictional Policy: Account Security

Customers should never share their one-time password (OTP), PIN, or password with anyone, including a bank employee.

If a customer suspects unauthorized account access, they should immediately use the official banking app or the verified customer-support channel to secure the account.

The bank will never ask a customer to disclose their full password or OTP over a phone call, email, or chat.

Customers should review account activity regularly and report unfamiliar transactions through the official support channel.
