# Fictional Policy: Digital Transfers

Before confirming a digital transfer, customers should verify the recipient name, destination details, and amount.

Transfer limits and processing times can depend on the payment method, account type, and applicable bank rules.

If a transfer appears pending, customers should check the official transaction status before initiating another transfer.

If a customer believes a transfer was unauthorized, they should report it promptly through the official banking app or verified customer-support channel.
